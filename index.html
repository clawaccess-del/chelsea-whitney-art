About Chelsea Whitney Art
A local creative presence shaped by Rogue Valley light, texture, wildlife, and feeling.

Chelsea Whitney Art is a local creative business centered on photography, artwork, and meaningful visual storytelling. The work is shaped by a love of wildlife, a close attention to detail, and a connection to the landscapes and natural rhythms of Southern Oregon.

From personal commissions to future sessions, prints, and creative collaborations, the studio is built to grow while staying rooted in a genuine, artist-led point of view.

Grounded in Southern Oregon Medford, Ashland, Jacksonville, and the surrounding Rogue Valley all fit naturally into the story.
Open to growth This can expand into a fuller booking site and portfolio archive without losing its softer, artist-led character.
Focused on meaningful imagery Wildlife, macro subjects, and atmospheric artwork set the tone for a portfolio that feels calm, expressive, and personal.
